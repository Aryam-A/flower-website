Your Guide to Flower Care

Tips to maintain the freshness and beauty of your flowers for as long as possible.

water_drop

Water: The Lifeline

Water is the most important element for keeping flowers alive. Frequency and quantity are key.

  • check_circle Change water every two days to keep it clean.
  • check_circle Use lukewarm water for faster absorption.
  • check_circle Cut stems at a 45-degree angle to increase absorption surface.

Ideal Lighting

Light is food, but excessive heat is the enemy of delicate petals.

Preferred Spot

Bright, indirect light near a window is best.

Avoid Heat

Keep away from direct sunlight or hot AC vents.
